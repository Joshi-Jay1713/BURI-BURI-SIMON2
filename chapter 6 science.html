D. Answer the Following Questions in Brief
1. Define the term ‘fuel’:
Substances which produce heat on burning in air are called fuels.
2. Name any two renewable natural sources of energy:
Wind energy and water energy.
3. Define the term ‘carbonisation’:
The process of conversion of dead plant materials into coal is called carbonisation.

Coke, Coal Tar & Coal Gas.

E. Answer the Following Questions
1. Describe, in brief, the process of ‘coal formation’:
Coal was formed from plant remains buried deep under the Earth’s crust. Over a large period of time, the chemical and physical properties of these remains changed due to geological action, resulting in the formation of coal.

3. How do petroleum and natural gas occur in nature?
Petroleum is found at moderate depths (500m – 2000m) between layers of impervious rocks. It is usually found mixed with salt water and floats above it due to being lighter. Natural gas is found above petroleum, trapped between the rock cap and the petroleum layer.
4. State the main advantages of using natural gas:
  • Burns readily and has high calorific value
  • Easy to carry and store
  • Undergoes complete combustion and is easily controllable
  • Cleaner fuel; releases fewer pollutants
  • Does not leave ash particles after burning
5. Why should coal and petroleum be used judiciously?
Coal and petroleum are limited natural resources that take millions of years to form. As non-renewable sources, they must be used carefully to ensure availability for future generations and to avoid depletion.
